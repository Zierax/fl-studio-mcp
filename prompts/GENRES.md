# GENRES — Style Specs

Numeric starting points, not templates. Copy the block you need into the
[recipe card](RECIPE.md), then adapt: the BPM to the room, the roles to the
channels that actually exist, the section map to the requested length.

If the brief contradicts a spec, **the brief wins** and the spec shrinks to
reference material.

Related: [RECIPE.md](RECIPE.md), [MIXING.md](MIXING.md),
[THEORY.md](THEORY.md).

## Dark trap

- **BPM** 140–160 (sweet spot 146) — half-time feel at 70–80
- **Key** minor, tonic-first cells (root, ♭3, 4); flattened 2nd is the
  signature "off" note
- **Drums** hard kick on every beat except bar 3 of each 8; snare on 2 and
  4 layered under a clap 4 dB down; 16th hats with 50% velocity variation
  and 10–14% swing; one open hat accent per bar
- **808** answers the kick rather than doubling it; slide into every 4th
  downbeat; LP at ~120 Hz; a parallel distorted layer for aggression
- **Melody** sparse, angry rests, one repeating hook cell per hook section;
  one note a quarter-tone flat once per 8 bars
- **Texture** vinyl noise bed, reversed riser into section changes
- **Mix** aggressive parallel compression on the bus; kick + 808 centred
  and tight; hats wide; lead dry with a short plate
- **Structure** no long intro — hook within 16 bars; a 2–4 bar breather
  before the biggest drop

## Phonk (cowbell / drift)

- **BPM** 130–150 (sweet spot 138)
- **Key** minor; pentatonic vocal-ish leads, heavy drift
- **Drums** 3-hit phrase loops (kick–snare–kick, or kick–clap), heavy
  swing (18–28%), cowbell 16ths or a driving off-beat layer
- **808** long notes with rapid pitch bends, long slides
- **Melody** chopped-vocal style hooks, repeated with small variations
- **Mix** intentionally distorted master; hats and cowbell bright and up;
  low end wide and blown out

## Rage / hyper-rap

- **BPM** 140–165 (sweet spot 150)
- **Drums** kick on every beat with syncopated breaks; layered snare/clap;
  riser into silence phrases (the drop *is* the silence)
- **Melody** one-note-with-motion leads, saw-stack chords, brass accents,
  vocal chops
- **Mix** parallel distortion, minimal reverb on leads, enormous centred
  kick, aggressive high-pass on everything but the kick

## Boom bap

- **BPM** 85–100 (sweet spot 92)
- **Key** minor with a 7th or 9th; sample-based, so the sample defines the
  key — plan around it
- **Drums** boom-bap swing (55–62%), snare slightly behind the grid, kick
  soft and round, hats on the "and" of 2 and 4
- **Bass** upright, mid-forward, walking, often a root+fifth cell
- **Melody** sparse, soul-influenced, wide reverb
- **Mix** dusty, low-passed top, everything slightly mono, gentle bus
  compression

## Drill

- **BPM** 140–150 (sweet spot 142–145), usually half-time
- **Key** minor, sliding chromatic bass
- **Drums** sliding 808 that drags the whole mix, hard snare on 3, minimal
  hats, half-time with occasional double-time fills
- **Melody** eerie, wide, minor 2nds, long held notes
- **Mix** the 808 and the hats compete for space — pick one to own the
  low-mid; heavy sidechain feel

## UK garage / 2-step

- **BPM** 130–138 (sweet spot 132–136)
- **Key** minor 7ths and 9ths
- **Drums** swung 16th hats, kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, snare on 2, a
  syncopated two-step pattern
- **Bass** rubbery sub with a rhythmic hook; the bassline *is* the track
- **Melody** chopped vocal, pitched, one repeated hook
- **Mix** everything slightly detuned and filtered; the hat pattern must
  be dead on the swing

## House (deep / tribal)

- **BPM** 120–128
- **Drums** four-on-the-floor, open hat on the offbeat, clap on 2 and 4,
  shaker 16ths; percussion layers
- **Bass** rolling, sidechained, moving every 8 bars
- **Melody** stabs and pads, filter sweeps on the transition
- **Mix** sidechain pump is mandatory on everything but the kick; the
  master is glued but not squashed

## Techno (peak-time / hypnotic)

- **BPM** 130–145
- **Drums** relentless, 16th hats with accents, kick on every beat, rides
  or a metallic layer for texture
- **Structure** one idea per 32 bars, changed by *one* parameter
  (filter, length, rhythm), 4–8 bar resets
- **Mix** no reverb on the drums, everything dry and close; the kick is
  the loudest thing by far

## Lo-fi / chillhop

- **BPM** 70–90 (sweet spot 82)
- **Key** major 7ths or minor with 9ths
- **Drums** soft, dusty, swing 50–60%, no sharp transients
- **Melody** Rhodes-like chords, simple 3–4 note phrases, lots of space
- **Mix** low-passed, tape wobble, vinyl crackle, everything at −6 to
  −12 dB relative to the drums; no limiter

## Ambient / cinematic

- **BPM** 60–90, or free time
- **Key** modal or drifting, no functional harmony for long stretches
- **Structure** 3–5 movements, each with one sustained gesture
- **Mix** long reverbs, no transient anywhere, high-pass the bass under
  80 Hz, wide and soft; loudness low (−20 LUFS is fine)

## Cinematic trailer

- **BPM** 90–120, ostinato-driven
- **Drums** big taiko-style hits, impacts on the downbeat of every 4
- **Texture** brass swells, braams, granular risers, choir pads
- **Structure** build → drop → break → bigger drop; every 8 bars adds one
  layer, and the drop removes all of them for one bar
- **Mix** sub-heavy, wide, sidechained pads, everything at 0 dBFS except
  the pads

## Afrobeats

- **BPM** 100–115 (sweet spot 108)
- **Key** minor with a flat 7th, or Dorian
- **Drums** log-drum pattern with a three-stroke ostinato, shaker on 16ths
  with a lazy swing, kick supporting the log drum rather than replacing it
- **Melody** short, repeating, call-and-response, layered vocals
- **Mix** the log drum sits forward; the bass is warm and round; leave
  space for the shaker; percussion panned in a circle

## House-pop / melodic

- **BPM** 118–126
- **Drums** four-on-the-floor, claps on 2 and 4, open hat before the drop
- **Melody** one big hook, sung, repeated with a chorus answer a third
  above
- **Mix** the vocal/lead is the loudest element; the kick is close but
  not dominant; everything above 10 kHz is gentle

## Cross-genre constants

Whatever the genre:

- **BPM**: choose so that the *half-time* interpretation lands on a bar
  boundary. 146, 150, 140 all work; 143 usually does not.
- **Swing**: 0% is correct for house/techno, 10–14% for trap, 18–28% for
  phonk/UKG, 50–62% for boom bap and lo-fi.
- **Key**: minor is the default, but modal or major-with-7ths is the
  escape hatch that keeps a track from sounding like the genre.
- **Energy**: every track needs at least one section below 3/10 and one
  above 8/10. A track that lives at 5–7 the whole way is flat.
