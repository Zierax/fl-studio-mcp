# STYLES — Ready Style Recipes

Adapt any recipe to the user's request. These are musical specs, not rigid
templates — when the user's instruction conflicts, the instruction wins and
the recipe shrinks to "reference material".

---

## Dark Trap (Lil Darkie / undertone-heavy)

- **BPM**: 140–160 (recipe sweet spot: 146)
- **Key**: minor, dark; tonic-first melodic cells (root, minor 3rd, 4th)
- **Drums**: hard kick + snare on beat 2 & 4; 16th hats with 50% velocity
  variation; one open hat accent per bar; percs ghosted behind
- **808**: slides welcome, following the kick, sub-heavy (roll off above
  ~120 Hz), distort a parallel layer for aggression
- **Melody**: sparse, angry rests, an off-key note in a calculated spot,
  one repeating hook cell every chorus
- **Texture**: ambient dirt (vinyl noise, airy vox chant), reversed riser
  into section changes
- **Mix**: aggressive parallel compression; hats wide; kick + 808 tight in
  the center; vocal chant sitting mid, wet
- **Structure guidance**: no long intros; hook within 16 bars; energy
  spikes at chorus, near-silence breathers before them

## Phonk (cowbell / drift)

- **BPM**: 130–150 (sweet spot: 138)
- **Key**: minor; pentatonic vocal-ish leads
- **Drums**: 3-hit phrase loops (kick-snare-kick / clap), heavy swing,
  cowbell 16ths or a driving off-beat layer
- **808**: long notes with rapid pitch bends; phone-vocal hooks
- **Mix**: distorted master (intentionally), hats and cowbell up and bright

## Rage (Playboi Carti / heavy synth)

- **BPM**: 140–165 (sweet spot: 150)
- **Drums**: loud everything; kick on every beat with syncopation breaks;
  snare/clap layers; riser + silence phrases
- **Melody**: one-note-with-motion leads, saw-stack chords, horn/brass
  accents; vocal chops
- **Mix**: parallel distortion, minimal reverb on leads, huge center kick

## Recipe card format (fill per request)

```
STYLE:        <name>
BPM:          <number>
KEY/SCALE:    <tonic + mode>
SOUND ROLES:  kick | snare/clap | 808 | hats | op-hat | perc | lead | pad | vocal
ARRANGEMENT:  <section map with bar counts + energy curve>
MIX TARGETS:  <per role: level, pan, EQ, FX>
SIGNATURE MOVE: <one deliberate surprise element per section change>
```

Keep the recipe card to **one short block** — mechanics, not essays.
